Privacy Policy for Hub City Transit

Effective Date: June 4, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Hub City Transit ("the App") requests access to your device's Location Data (GPS) to provide real-time bus tracking and distance calculations. We do not store your location data on our servers. Your location is processed locally on your device or ephemerally to request nearby transit stops.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your location data is used exclusively for:

  • Showing your current position on the map relative to bus routes.
  • Calculating walking distance and estimated time of arrival (ETA) to bus stops.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information or location data to outside parties. All transit data is provided as a public service.

4. Your Consent

By using our app, you consent to our privacy policy. You may revoke location permissions at any time via your device's system settings.

5. Contact Us

If there are any questions regarding this privacy policy, you may contact the Hub City Transit administration team.
