What this app is

Hub City Transit is an unofficial app for viewing Hub City Transit bus routes, timetables, live vehicle positions and trip plans in Hattiesburg, Mississippi. It is provided free of charge.

This app is not operated by Hub City Transit or the City of Hattiesburg. It is an independent app built on publicly available transit data. For official information, contact the agency directly.

Accuracy of information

Timetables, route maps and service alerts come from published transit data and are provided as-is. Live bus positions come from the agency's public vehicle feed and may be delayed, missing, or wrong.

Arrival estimates are estimates. They are calculated from scheduled times and, where available, the current position of the bus. They are not a guarantee that a bus will arrive at any particular time.

Do not rely on this app alone where being late has consequences. Allow extra time and check with the agency if a trip is critical.

Trip planning

Suggested trips are generated from the published timetable and walking distance estimates. Walking directions are provided by Mapbox. Routes suggested by this app may not account for road closures, construction, accessibility needs, or personal safety. Use your own judgement.

Service availability

The app depends on services we do not control, including the agency's vehicle feed, map tiles, and address search. Any of these may be unavailable, and the app may show stale or incomplete information as a result.
